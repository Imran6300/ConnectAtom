01 BRAND


Who ConnectAtom is
ConnectAtom makes ready-to-use IoT monitoring. Small sensors watch the things that cost money when
they fail, and people hear about a problem from ConnectAtom before it becomes a loss.

Positioning
For owners and managers of cold rooms, clinics, kitchens, warehouses and small factories, ConnectAtom is the
ready-to-use monitoring system that sends a clear alert the moment something drifts, so it is fixed before it becomes a
loss. Unlike generic IoT kits and heavy enterprise platforms, it arrives as a working product with install help, local support
and one simple monthly plan.

Voice
• Plain words, short sentences. Numbers beat adjectives: 'Alert in under a minute' beats 'lightning-fast'.
• Avoid hype and filler: cutting-edge, revolutionary, next-gen, seamless, solutions, synergy.
• Write alerts like a calm colleague: what happened, how long, what to check.

Naming rules
• Write ConnectAtom: one word, capital C and A. Never 'Connect Atom', 'Connectatom' or 'CONNECTATOM' in running
  text.
• Products use the brand plus a plain name: ConnectAtom Cold Room, ConnectAtom Med Fridge, ConnectAtom Kitchen,
  ConnectAtom Godown.
• Model codes: CA, two letters, generation number (CA-CR1 cold room, CA-MF1 med fridge, CA-KT1 kitchen, CA-GD1
  godown). Serials use CA-000123, matching the platform device ID.

Boilerplate (about 50 words)
ConnectAtom builds ready-to-use wireless monitoring for cold rooms, clinics, kitchens, warehouses and small factories
in India. Sensors send live readings to a simple dashboard and alert the right person on WhatsApp the moment something
drifts. Every system comes with installation help and a clear monthly plan.

The mark and the wordmark
An open ring is the atom's orbit and the C of Connect. The nucleus is the sensor at the core. The trace
and end node carry data out, like a circuit-board trace ending in a pad. One simple shape, flat colour, no
gradients, so it reproduces equally well on a screen, a label and an engraved enclosure.

Before you rely on the name and logo commercially, run a trademark search in India (class 9 for devices, class 42 for software

tell you whether something similar is already registered.

Status and chart colours
• Status: OK green, Signal Amber, Alert Red. On light backgrounds use the darker text tones (green 157A47, amber
  8A5300, red C2362F, all 5.4:1 or better) so text stays readable. Always pair colour with a word or icon, never colour
  alone.
• Charts, in order on light: 0A8F80, 3B82F6, F59E0B, 8B5CF6, EF4444, 64748B. On dark: 14D1BC, 5B9DFF, FFB224,
  A78BFA, FF7A77, 94A3B8.
• Print: match colours from a physical proof with your printer. The values here are screen (RGB) values.

Search and sharing details
• Title pattern: Product benefit | ConnectAtom, for example 'Cold room temperature monitoring with WhatsApp
  alerts | ConnectAtom'.
• Logo alt text: 'ConnectAtom'. Use the SVG, not a PNG, in the header. Set favicon.svg, 192 and 512 px icons and the
  1200x630 share image from the kit.
• Add Organisation structured data with the logo URL so search engines can show it.
